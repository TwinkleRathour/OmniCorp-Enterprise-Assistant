# Corporate Travel & Reimbursement Policy (OmniCorp 2026)

## 1. Domestic Travel Allowances
- Tier 1 Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad): Hotel accommodation up to ₹8,000 per night inclusive of taxes.
- Tier 2 & Other Cities: Hotel accommodation up to ₹5,000 per night inclusive of taxes.
- Daily Food Allowance (Per-Diem): Flat ₹1,500 per day for official travel outside the base location lasting over 8 hours. Original tax invoices required.

## 2. Flight and Rail Bookings
- Standard domestic air travel must be Economy Class, booked at least 7 days prior via the corporate travel desk.
- Rail Travel: AC 2-Tier or Executive Chair Car is permitted.
- Business Class airfare is strictly restricted to Vice Presidents and above, or flights with scheduled duration exceeding 6 continuous hours.

## 3. Reimbursement Deadlines & Approvals
- All reimbursement claims must be submitted via the corporate portal within 15 calendar days of trip conclusion.
- Late submissions require secondary authorization from the Finance Director.
- Mileage for official use of personal vehicle: ₹12 per km for 4-wheelers, ₹6 per km for 2-wheelers.
