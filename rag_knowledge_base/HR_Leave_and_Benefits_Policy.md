# Corporate Leave & Employee Benefits Policy (OmniCorp 2026)

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement
- All full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Leaves accrue at the rate of 1.5 days per completed calendar month of active service.
- Up to 8 unused annual leaves can be carried forward to the following calendar year. Any additional unused leaves lapse automatically on December 31.
- Leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days require direct manager sign-off at least 7 calendar days in advance.

## 2. Sick and Medical Leave
- Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave annually, credited on January 1.
- Medical certificates signed by a registered practitioner are mandatory for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
- Unused sick leaves cannot be encashed or carried forward.

## 3. Parental Leave
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks of fully paid leave for female employees, applicable for up to two surviving children.
- Paternity Leave: 10 working days of fully paid leave, valid within 6 months of childbirth or legal adoption.

## 4. Hybrid Work and Attendance
- Core working hours: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time.
- Standard hybrid model permits up to 2 remote days per week with line manager approval.
- Full remote status requires quarterly approval from Department Head and HR.
