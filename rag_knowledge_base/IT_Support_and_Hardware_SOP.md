# IT Operations & Hardware Standard Operating Procedure (OmniCorp 2026)

## 1. Hardware Entitlement & Lifecycle
- Standard Issue: High-performance workstation or laptop (Dell Latitude or HP Pavilion/EliteBook class).
- Hardware Refresh Cycle: Every 36 months from initial issue date.
- Battery replacements are authorized outside refresh cycles if battery diagnostic health falls below 60%.

## 2. IT Helpdesk Incident Categories & SLAs
- Priority 1 (System Down / Blocker): Response within 2 hours, target resolution within 6 hours.
- Priority 2 (VPN / Network / Wi-Fi Authentication Failure): Response within 4 hours, target resolution within 12 hours.
- Priority 3 (Peripherals / Software Licensing / General Access): Response within 24 hours.

## 3. Wi-Fi and Network Connectivity SOP
- Secure SSID: `OmniCorp-Secure`
- Authentication Protocol: WPA3-Enterprise using corporate Microsoft Entra ID single sign-on credentials.
- Troubleshooting Checklist:
  1. Toggle Wi-Fi adapter off and on.
  2. Forget network profile and re-authenticate.
  3. Ensure corporate GlobalProtect VPN client is updated.
  4. If unresolved, raise an IT Helpdesk ticket including your device MAC address and Asset Tag.
